# Chapter 3 dimensional-study evidence selection

## Scope

This folder contains compact, final evidence copied from:

`${PROJECT_ROOT}/analysis\dimension_matched_pof_study_20260726\results\matched_150eval_5seed_v2_20260726`

The full run, probe, and prediction MAT files remain in that source study
directory. The compact copies here are sufficient to audit every new number,
caption, and inclusion decision in `geometry_based_binary_feasibility_paper_v4.tex`.

## Retained in the chapter

1. `fig_v4_clipped_gp_paired_dimension_metrics`: a portrait-friendly 2-by-2
   figure showing the shipped clipped-GP field's FPR, FNR, balanced error, and
   AUC. It retains all five paired trajectories and medians.
2. `fig_v4_clipped_gp_d4_ranked_score_truth`: a portrait-friendly qualitative
   comparison of one deterministic representative D4 run per problem. It is
   explicitly identified as illustrative rather than dimension-trend evidence.
3. A compact six-row paired-change table based on
   `dimension_trend_paired_summary.csv`, with prevalence interpretation from
   `dimension_trend_problem_method_medians.csv`.
4. The 14-of-15 paired GP balanced-error result, FNR-dominated error
   asymmetry, CF1 prevalence control, C2-DTLZ2/MW7 prevalence confounding,
   offline-RF counterexample, WB D4 anchor, and 24-of-24 validation result.

## Excluded from the main chapter

1. `all_problem_gp_rf_metrics.png`: duplicates the paired table/figure and
   does not connect matched replicate pairs clearly.
2. `paired_dimension_gp_rf_metrics.png`: the original 2-by-4 landscape
   composite is not legible at A4 portrait text width. RF values are retained
   in the compact table as offline formulation sensitivity.
3. The four original `*_pf_gp_rf_rank_truth_confusion.png` figures: their
   4.3-to-1 layout is not legible in A4 portrait and they mix objective-space,
   GP, and offline-RF evidence. The retained D4 matrix removes this redundancy.
4. The 11-column absolute-metric table: too wide for the main chapter. Exact
   medians remain in `dimension_trend_problem_method_medians.csv`.
5. `dimension_trend_spearman.csv`: heterogeneous problem families and strong
   prevalence confounding make a global dimension correlation unsafe.
6. Headline domain error, ECE, FDR, clipping mass, and ambiguous-mass results:
   these are retained in `field_metrics.csv` but do not sharpen the tested
   dimension claim.

## Interpretation boundary

- The RF proxy was fitted offline to GP-selected final datasets. It is not an
  online RF optimizer comparison.
- Paired changes are medians of within-replicate high-minus-low differences;
  they are not differences of marginal medians.
- CF1 is the least prevalence-confounded contrast. C2-DTLZ2 and MW7 are stress
  tests because feasible volume declines sharply.
- Five pairs support a descriptive engineering screen, not a statistical-
  significance, causal-dimension, universal-PoF, or optimizer-performance
  claim.
